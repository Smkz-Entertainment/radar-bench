# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Radar Bench welcoming, respectful, and free
from harassment regardless of identity, background, ability, or experience.
We value careful technical disagreement, reproducible evidence, and respect for
the safety and privacy implications of executing third-party code.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive behavior include empathy, precise feedback, accepting
responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the project and
its community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized or
derogatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, and
publishing credentials, secrets, or working exploit details in public channels.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project
publicly. Community leaders may remove or reject content that violates these
standards and will communicate reasons where appropriate. Report conduct
concerns privately through the [security contact](https://github.com/Smkz-Entertainment/radar-bench/security/advisories/new)
when the report contains sensitive information; use a sanitized public issue for
non-sensitive community concerns.

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with reporter privacy
respected. Corrective action may include a private correction, warning,
temporary ban, or permanent ban.
