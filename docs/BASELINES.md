# Baselines

`static-v0.4` is the immutable static attribution artifact. `naive-deterministic` is the simple deterministic planner used as a lower-bound comparison. `agentic-v0.5-frozen` is the unchanged investigator from commit `60ccc18`.

Baselines are reference implementations, not targets to tune. Their source paths, commit, and expected results are locked by the suite metadata and release provenance.
