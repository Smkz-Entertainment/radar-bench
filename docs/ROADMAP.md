# Roadmap

v0.1 through v0.3 are frozen. v0.4 is Gold Corpus Mining & Admission and has
one purpose: establish a real, temporally separated corpus before capability
claims or product work.

The v0.4 sequence is:

1. Mine and admit the 20/40 pilot through resolution chains and explicit
   rejection reasons.
2. Stop on the pilot continuation thresholds when action-owner precision or
   candidate-induced precision is below its required floor.
3. Only after a passing deterministic pilot expand to the 50/120 and
   120/300 stages.
4. Keep local-model and Codex comparisons out of corpus admission until the
   temporal and safety contracts are genuinely established.

The v0.5 sequence is a bounded continuation experiment over the frozen v0.4
records:

1. Build temporal-blind InvestigationEpisodes and classify attributable,
   externally dependent, and genuinely unattributable cases from historical
   gold evidence.
2. Execute the deterministic heuristic lane through the replay oracle with a
   five-substantive-experiment budget and no owner guessing.
3. Publish candidate-induced, component, owner, abstention, Resolution@k,
   safety, efficiency, ablation, and temporal-blindness evidence.
4. Continue only when the frozen v0.5 gates pass; stop and abstain when the
   kill criteria trigger. Optional local-model and Codex lanes remain blocked
   without suitable no-cost availability or authorized credentials.

Only after v0.3 passes should shadow-mode ingestion, a public corpus/dashboard,
or other user-facing Radar product work be considered. No GitHub bot or
maintainer-contact integration is planned.
