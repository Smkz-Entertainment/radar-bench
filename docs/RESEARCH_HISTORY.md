# Research history

Radar Bench preserves the v0.1–v0.7 research sequence:

- v0.1–v0.3 established offline schemas, snapshots, admission, and temporal separation;
- v0.4 measured a static pilot and exposed weak owner attribution;
- v0.5 froze an interactive investigator for a replay comparison;
- v0.6 challenged oracle integrity and exposed gold leakage, so replay certification was rejected;
- v0.7 sealed executable cases and ran the decisive 5 + 20 experiment.

The v0.5 apparent replay success is therefore not a product result. The v0.6 leakage finding invalidated that oracle, and v0.7 supplied the executable evidence needed to falsify the agentic attribution thesis without changing the investigator.
