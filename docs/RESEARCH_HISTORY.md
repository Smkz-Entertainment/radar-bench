# Research history

Radar Bench preserves the research sequence without treating earlier
iterations as current executable evidence.

- v0.1-v0.7 established the schemas, safety twins, frozen baselines, and the
  fail-closed interpretation of missing historical inputs.
- v1.0.0 preserved the original decisive run at immutable tag `v1.0.0`.
  That record is historical evidence and is explicitly not runtime evidence.
- v1.0.1 corrects the sealed A03 reproducer and gives the corrected executable
  suite the immutable identity `decisive-v1.1`. It does not rewrite v1.0.0.
- Artifact publication and runtime reconstruction established that acquisition
  and Docker execution can be tested independently. The final benchmark claim
  remains gated on clean-clone replay and strict result parity.

The product hypothesis is not promoted by a passing infrastructure check: the
frozen investigator's decisive result remains the scientific conclusion.
