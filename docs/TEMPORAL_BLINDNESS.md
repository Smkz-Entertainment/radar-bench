# Temporal blindness

Every case is divided into T0 incident evidence, Tcut candidate-visible evidence, and Tgold evaluator-only evidence. Candidate runtime manifests cannot mount gold labels, historical discussions, maintainer confirmation, or post-cutoff fixes. A later explanation may be used to score a prediction, but never to produce the candidate observation.

The old replay oracle failed this boundary in v0.6. Replay outputs remain historical research artifacts and are not accepted as v1 certification evidence.
